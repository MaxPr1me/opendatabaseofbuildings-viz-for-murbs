# Research Questions

## Primary Research Questions

### RQ1: Building Population and Classification
How can MURBs be reliably identified from the Statistics Canada Open Database of Buildings given heterogeneous source data, incomplete attributes, and varying classification systems?

### RQ2: Typical MURB Size
What are defensible statistical distributions for Canadian MURB ground-level footprint area, floor-plate area, gross floor area, building length, building width, storeys, height, unit count, and units per floor?

### RQ3: Building Form and Aspect Ratio
What are the characteristic geometric properties of Canadian MURBs including aspect ratio, orientation, compactness, rectangularity, convexity, and facade dimensions?

### RQ4: Shape Classification
Can building footprints be reliably classified into simulation-oriented shape families (rectangle, slab, L, T, U, H, courtyard, tower, podium-and-tower, etc.)?

### RQ5: Mid-Rise and High-Rise Definitions
What storey/height boundaries meaningfully distinguish mid-rise from high-rise MURBs for simulation purposes, considering building-code thresholds, construction-system changes, and energy-performance transitions?

### RQ6: Window-to-Wall Ratio
How should WWR be handled when building footprints alone cannot provide facade glazing information? What defensible archetypal assumptions or external data sources can support orientation-specific WWR for simulation?

### RQ7: Representative Archetypes
How can representative MURB geometries be derived from the national building population for use in OpenStudio/EnergyPlus building-performance simulations?

## Secondary Research Questions

### RQ8: Geographic Variation
How do MURB geometry characteristics vary by province, climate zone, urban context, and construction era?

### RQ9: Data Quality and Representativeness
Is the building-footprint sample representative of the national MURB stock? What biases exist due to uneven source coverage?

### RQ10: Simulation Suitability
What validation is needed to ensure exported geometries are suitable for EnergyPlus simulation via gbXML or OpenStudio Model format?
